# FinancialTracker

Freed — how it works
A personal finance tracker with five screens: overview, spending, wealth, retirement, and insights. All data is saved to your account and syncs across devices.
Getting started
1
Create your account
Open the app and tap Create one. Enter your email and a password (min. 6 characters). A confirmation email will be sent — click the link to verify, then sign in.
2
Set your monthly income
On the Overview screen, tap the income figure (shows R 0 initially). Enter your take-home pay for the month. Do this each month — income is tracked per month.
3
Set up your categories
Go to Spending → Categories. Six default categories are loaded. Tap any to edit or delete, or tap + Category to add your own. Set a monthly budget for each so progress bars work correctly.
4
Log your first expense
Tap + Add Expense on the Overview screen. Enter the amount, choose a category, add an optional note, and confirm the date. It will appear on the dashboard immediately.
5
Add investments & savings goals
Go to Wealth. Tap + Add under Investments (e.g. TFSA, RA, shares) and + Goal under Savings Goals (e.g. emergency fund). Enter current values and monthly contributions — these feed into your net worth and insights.
6
Configure the retirement planner
Go to Retire. Enter your current age, target retirement age, monthly contribution, expected annual return, and freedom target (the lump sum you want to retire on). The projected fund and growth chart update live.
The five screens
▦
Overview
Net worth, freedom progress, monthly spend vs income, 6-month chart, and categories with spend this month.
≡
Spending
Three tabs — Categories (edit/delete), Transactions (full log), and Budgets (remaining per category).
↗
Wealth
Track investments by type and value, and savings goals with target vs current and monthly contribution.
◷
Retire
Compound interest calculator projecting your retirement fund with a year-by-year growth chart.
✦
Insights
Auto-generated observations about your savings rate, budget overruns, monthly balance, portfolio value, and nearest savings goal. Updates as you add data.
Things to know
◂▸
Navigate months using the arrows in the top bar. Each month is independent — income and expenses are tracked separately per month.
✕
Delete a transaction by tapping it in the Transactions tab and confirming. Categories can also be deleted from the category edit screen.
☁
Data saves automatically to Supabase within a second of any change. Sign in on another device and your data is there.
⊕
Install as an app — in Chrome on Android tap the menu and choose "Add to Home Screen". On iOS use Safari's Share button then "Add to Home Screen".
R
Currency is ZAR (R) throughout. All amounts are stored as whole numbers rounded to the nearest rand.
