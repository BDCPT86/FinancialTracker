User Guide · v2.0

Freed Finance

Everything you need to track spending, manage investments, eliminate debt and plan your retirement.

Getting started

Freed is a PWA — install it on your phone from your browser. All data is encrypted and stored securely in your personal Supabase account.

1
Create your account
Open the app and tap Create one. Enter your email and a password (min 6 characters). Click the confirmation link in your email, then sign in.
2
Set your monthly income
Tap the income figure on the Overview screen (shows R 0). Enter your take-home pay for the month. Repeat each month — income is tracked per month independently.
3
Set up categories and budgets
Go to Spending → Categories. Six default categories are pre-loaded. Tap any to set a monthly budget amount, or tap + Category to add your own.
4
Add recurring transactions
Go to Spending → Budgets. Tap + Add under Recurring Transactions. Add salary, rent, subscriptions — these auto-apply on the 1st of each month.
5
Add investments and savings goals
Go to Wealth. Add investments (TFSA, RA, shares) and savings goals (emergency fund, holiday). These feed your net worth calculation.
6
Add your liabilities
In the Wealth screen, add any outstanding debt — home loan, vehicle finance, credit card. This completes your true net worth picture.

Recurring transactions

Recurring transactions are the most time-saving feature in Freed. Instead of manually re-entering salary, rent, subscriptions and other fixed monthly items every month, you set them up once and they appear automatically.

Recurring transactions are stored once and auto-applied on first open each month
↻
How to set up a recurring transaction
Step by step
1
Go to Spending → Budgets tab
The Budgets tab is the third tab on the Spending screen. Scroll to the bottom to find the Recurring Transactions section.
2
Tap + Add
A modal slides up. Select the type (Expense or Income), enter the amount, choose a category (for expenses), and give it a description like "Rent" or "Salary".
3
Save the recurring transaction
Tap Save Recurring. It will appear in the list with a ↻ monthly badge. It is now stored permanently in your account.
4
It auto-applies from now on
Every time you open the app in a new month, Freed checks if your recurring transactions have been added yet. If not, it adds them automatically, dated the 1st of the month. They appear in your transaction list with a ↻ symbol.
💡
Good candidates for recurring transactions: salary, rent/mortgage payment, car instalment, gym membership, streaming subscriptions, school fees, medical aid contributions. Essentially anything that comes out every month at the same amount.
⚠️
To delete a recurring transaction: go to Spending → Budgets, scroll to the Recurring Transactions list, and tap the item. This removes the template — it does not delete past transactions that were already applied.
🔄
Auto-apply only runs once per month per recurring item. It checks whether a transaction with that recurring ID already exists in the current month before adding it — so opening the app multiple times won't create duplicates.

Liabilities and debt

Adding your liabilities is critical for an accurate net worth. Without them, your net worth is overstated by the full amount of your debt.

Net worth = total assets − total liabilities · Add all your debts for an accurate picture
🏦
How to add a liability
Wealth screen
1
Go to the Wealth screen
Tap Wealth in the nav. Scroll down past Investments and Savings Goals to find the Liabilities & Debt section.
2
Tap + Add and fill in the details
Enter a name (e.g. Home Loan — FNB), select the type, enter the outstanding balance, the annual interest rate (e.g. 11.75), and the minimum monthly payment.
3
The net worth updates immediately
Your net worth on the Overview screen will decrease by the liability balance. The Insights screen will show your debt-to-asset ratio and total monthly interest cost.
📋
Types supported: Home Loan, Vehicle Finance, Credit Card, Personal Loan, Student Loan, Other. Each shows monthly interest cost calculated as balance × rate ÷ 12.

How calculations work

Savings rate

Your savings rate shows what percentage of your gross income you are directing into investments and savings goals via monthly contributions.

Savings rate = (Investment contributions + savings contributions) ÷ gross income × 100
This reflects deliberate savings — not leftover cash. A rate of 20%+ is considered healthy. The Insights screen will flag if you are below 10%.

True cash flow

The true cash flow insight shows your real discretionary surplus after both expenses and savings commitments are accounted for.

True cash flow = income − total expenses − savings/investment contributions
If this is negative, you are spending more than you earn even before savings — a warning sign. If it is positive, that is genuinely unallocated money.

Budget pace warning

In the current month, Freed projects your spend forward based on how much you have spent so far relative to how many days have passed.

Projected spend = (spend so far ÷ days elapsed) × days in month
If the projected spend exceeds your budget for a category, it is flagged as "Overspending pace" in the Insights screen even before you have actually gone over.

Retirement — nominal vs real value

The retirement planner shows two figures: the nominal projected value (what the number will say on paper) and the real value adjusted for inflation (what that money will actually be worth in today's purchasing power).

Always set your freedom target in today's money — Freed will tell you if your real projected value meets it
Real value = nominal FV ÷ (1 + inflation rate)^years
The default inflation rate is 5.5% (South African CPI average). Adjust it in the Retirement Settings panel. Your freedom target should be in today's money — e.g. "I need R 10m in today's purchasing power to retire".

Tips and things to know

◂▸
Navigate months using the arrows in the topbar. Each month is independent — income and expenses are tracked per month. Recurring transactions auto-apply when you switch to a new month.
☁
Auto-save: changes are written to Supabase roughly 800ms after your last edit. No save button needed. Your data syncs across all your devices automatically.
✕
Delete a transaction by tapping it in the Transactions tab. Tap a recurring transaction template in the Budgets tab to delete it. Tap any investment, saving, category or liability to edit or delete it.
💵
Set income by tapping the income figure (R 0) on the Overview screen. Income is per-month and must be set each month, unless you add your salary as a recurring income transaction.
📱
Install as an app — Android: Chrome menu → Add to Home Screen. iOS: Safari Share button → Add to Home Screen. The app requires HTTPS to install — it will not work from a local file.
🔐
Security: all data is stored in a Supabase database with Row Level Security enabled. Each user can only read and write their own row — not other users' data. Passwords are managed by Supabase Auth.
